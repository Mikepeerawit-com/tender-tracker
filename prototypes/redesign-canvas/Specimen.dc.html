Tender Tracker — the system

One signal colour, one alarm colour reserved for time, and one flag that describes a quote rather than a state. Everything else is ink. Nothing on any screen is told apart by colour alone.

Colour

Four hues, and what each one is allowed to say
Signal oklch(0.55 0.15 195) This needs you, or this is the one we chose. Primary actions, the selected quote, rank 1, the outstanding-for-you band.
Alarm oklch(0.55 0.15 28) Time, and only time. A missed submission, a passed deadline, an unsourced item after the internal date. It never touches a money figure.
Flag oklch(0.62 0.13 75) A property of a quote or a figure, not a state: Alternative, a provisional landed cost, a too-close-to-call ranking.
Ink oklch(0.20 0.014 235) Everything else. Ground is oklch(0.986 0.004 200) — a cool near-white, not cream — with hairlines at 0.905.
Why alarm never lands on money

In Chinese financial convention red is up and green is down — the inverse of the Western reading. A red negative margin would be read as a gain by half the people using this. Keeping alarm to deadlines sidesteps the inversion rather than picking a side of it.

Chinese is not English with different words Latin labels are uppercase with 0.055em tracking. Chinese has no case and tracking hurts it, so the CJK label is sentence-case at 11.5px instead — one rule per script, not one rule stretched over both.
The CJK face is chosen, not inherited PingFang SC, Hiragino Sans GB, Source Han Sans SC, Noto Sans SC, Microsoft YaHei — declared in the stack, drawn by the device. A web-loaded CJK face is megabytes over a phone network in a WeCom webview, so IBM Plex Sans was picked to sit beside PingFang rather than fight it.

The indicator lamp

The one device this design is remembered by — same shape, same meaning, on every screen
Lit, alarm
A date has gone by. Submission missed, sourcing overdue. Always paired with the sentence that says which date and how long ago.
Lit, signal
Something is expected of the person reading. Quotes due tomorrow, pick a quote, chase the client today.
Unlit
Live, nothing due. The lamp is drawn hollow rather than omitted so that a calm row and an urgent row are the same shape.
